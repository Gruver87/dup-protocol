## What “open on GitHub” means

Anyone may **view, clone, test, fork, and modify** this repository under MIT.
That is intentional: evidence, diligence, and community review need public
source.

Anyone may **not** honestly claim to be the original author of DUP Protocol
or present a copy as the official project without attribution. Removing
copyright / LICENSE / NOTICE, or rebranding a clone as if it were the
canonical work, violates the license conditions and misrepresents authorship.

**Technical fact:** published source cannot be made “un-copyable.” Protection
here is **copyright + MIT attribution + brand identity + public git provenance**.

## Required when redistributing

1. Keep the MIT copyright and permission notice ([`LICENSE`](../LICENSE)).
2. Keep or reproduce [`NOTICE`](../NOTICE) (or equivalent clear attribution).
3. Prefer citing [`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff) in papers / diligence packs.
